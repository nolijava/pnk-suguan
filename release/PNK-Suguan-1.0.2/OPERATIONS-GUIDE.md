# PNK Suguan 1.0.2 — Operations Guide

Use the existing application workflows for teachers, dako, availability, weekly scheduling, SUGO, RESERBA, RESERBA II, reports, and PDF generation.

Schedules follow DRAFT → FINALIZED → PUBLISHED. Finalized revision uses the authorized workflow; published schedules are locked except for SUPER_ADMIN correction. Weekly assignments do not mutate Current Destination or Destination History. Historical Backfill remains historical. RESERBA II does not deprive SUGO or RESERBA.

English dako cannot receive a Filipino teacher. `LANGUAGE_MISMATCH` is non-overridable. The accepted Weekly Suguan PDF keeps Page 1 unchanged and appends Patotoo ORIGINAL/DUPLICATE assignment pages for populated assignment rows.
